This is a place where I write about things I find interesting. Mostly systems architecture, cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, and whatever else catches my attention.

I'm a solutions architect and cloud engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I work on building scalable platforms and solving infrastructure problems at scale. This site is where I collect my thoughts, share what I've learned, and occasionally argue with myself about technical decisions.

## What You'll Find Here

Mostly engineering notes and essays. I write about:

- **Architecture**: System design decisions, tradeoffs, and patterns
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: GPU computing, multi-region deployments, Kubernetes
- **Distributed Systems**: Messaging, consistency, scaling challenges
- **Career Notes**: Lessons from building products from MVP to thousands of users

Not everything will be polished. Some posts will be rough notes. That's intentional. This is a working document of my thinking, not a publication.

## Why Write?

Writing forces clarity. You don't know what you think until you try to explain it. This site is primarily for myself. A place to externalize thinking and build a record of what I've learned.

If you find something useful, that's a bonus.
